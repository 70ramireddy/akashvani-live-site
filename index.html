What the app does

Akashvani Live collects the publicly available live streams that All India Radio publishes on its website and presents them in a simple player: pick a language, tap a station, and listen. Playback continues in the background with controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

What All India Radio is

All India Radio, officially known as Akashvani ("voice from the sky") since 2023, is India's national public radio broadcaster. It is operated by Prasar Bharati, the autonomous public service broadcaster established by an Act of Parliament under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting.

  • Founded in 1936, it is one of the largest radio networks in the world.
  • It broadcasts in 23 languages and more than 170 dialects, reaching almost the entire population of India.
  • National services include Vividh Bharati (film and light music), FM Rainbow, FM Gold and News 24x7, alongside hundreds of regional and local stations.
  • Live streams of these stations are published by Prasar Bharati at akashvani.gov.in; this app plays those public streams.
